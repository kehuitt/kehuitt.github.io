Research project associated with **Chinese Conference on Pattern Recognition and Computer Vision (PRCV 2025); proceedings published in 2026**.

## Core approach

Uses a diffusion-based preference score as a reward for trajectory prediction, together with rejection sampling.

A pretrained diffusion backbone scores trajectory features for alignment with human behavior. The score guides reinforcement learning and selection of training trajectories.

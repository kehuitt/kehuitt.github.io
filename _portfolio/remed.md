Research project associated with **International Joint Conference on Neural Networks (IJCNN)**.

## Core approach

Improves medical document retrieval through embedding fine-tuning for retrieval-augmented query answering.

The released implementation includes medical menu and paper datasets, embedding models, training, fine-tuning, and retrieval evaluation.
